How it works & sources

Fixtures come live from ESPN's public API (TheSportsDB as fallback). Israel uses Charlton's own TV schedule (sport1.maariv.co.il), loaded live. Other listings are refreshed every few hours by a GitHub Action: UK from live-footballontv.com; Israel, Singapore, USA, Australia, Canada and India from livesoccertv.com match pages. A listing always wins; otherwise a rule engine uses competition, kick-off day/slot (incl. the UK Saturday 3pm blackout), region rights and how many games kick off at the same time.

Your channels: Sky Sports & TNT Sports (UK, incl. 4K), TLC (only when a listing shows football on it), Charlton Sport 1–4 (+ Sport 6) and the Sport 5 family (Israel), all Singapore sports channels (StarHub Hub Premier / Hub Sports, beIN SG, Singtel mio). USA, Australia, Canada and India are shown for reference only (their main rights holders), not as your channels. Always double-check the broadcaster's own guide.
